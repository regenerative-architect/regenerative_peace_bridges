# Workshop handouts and posters — 3.24.0

Open a solution bridge and select **Print workshop / PDF**. Choose a discussion handout or workshop poster, then press **Print / Save PDF**. The final button opens the browser's normal print dialog: select a printer or **Save as PDF**. Browsers control the destination, filename and final pagination; this feature does not upload anything or use an online PDF service.

## Layouts

- **A4 or US Letter handout:** normally four numbered sheets covering the proposal, prerequisites, risks, safeguards and safer first step; six discussion questions with writing space; pilot steps, measures and a follow-up table; and related sources with limitations and readable URLs. Longer bridge content is split onto additional numbered sheets rather than truncated or squeezed into tiny type. The current library produces four to six designed pages.
- **A3 portrait poster:** one large-format sheet with the selected bridge's summary and first step, six workshop questions, safety/consent reminders and space for the next discussion date. It explicitly directs facilitators to the accompanying handout for complete prerequisites, risks, safeguards and sources. It is a discussion overview, not a substitute for the detailed handout.

Use portrait orientation, the indicated paper size, 100% scale and browser headers/footers turned off. Layout margins are provided by the template. A3 can be scaled to smaller paper when necessary, with reduced lettering; check legibility. Printer drivers, browser engines and paper settings can alter page breaks, so inspect the preview before producing many copies. The layouts use black text, rules and white backgrounds; background graphics are not needed.

The modal preview includes a readable expandable copy of the print content. It supports keyboard focus and Escape to close. Canceling or finishing printing restores the normal app. Printing directly from the browser while this preview is open uses the selected template. Outside the preview, existing app printing is unchanged. Existing planner briefs and meeting-kit printing remain separate.

## Workshop use

The six questions cover affected people and consent, safe access and rights, water/neighbors across conflict lines, fair resources and contingencies, knowledge/privacy, and learning/stop conditions. They are facilitation prompts applied to the selected bridge, not recorded answers or findings. The handout retains the bridge's own prerequisites, risks, safeguards, steps and measures so discussion stays specific to its proposal.

Allow people to pass or offer differing accounts. Attendance or a majority vote is not everyone's consent. Do not write private identities, exact sensitive locations or restricted knowledge on public materials. The printed sheets do not establish land clearance, verify rights, authenticate consent, or authorize field work. Follow qualified processes and the existing app safeguards when developing an actual plan.

## Privacy and language

Only the bundled public bridge and source registry is used. No saved plan name, local place, maturity assessment, consent history, inventory, workshop answer, private note or community knowledge account is read into these print templates. Choosing a bridge to print does not switch or change the active plan. Source grades remain grades of publications, not local outcomes.

The templates are explicitly **English** even when the surrounding interface is in another language. They use the original public registry rather than silently presenting draft translations as reviewed workshop material. Facilitation and any translation for local use should be reviewed by the relevant community. Paper copies and PDFs may circulate beyond the meeting: users control any handwritten additions and their subsequent sharing.

## Offline and compatibility

Works in the offline installed app and the self-contained HTML edition using the device's browser/print system. No remote fonts, account, telemetry or PDF server are required. Opening the original source URLs requires connectivity unless those documents were saved separately. Embedded preview mode explains that printing requires the downloadable app.

The upgrade adds no private data fields, migration or new backup format. Existing private backups, plan briefs and systems exports are unchanged. `workshop_print.js` is included in the versioned service-worker cache and standalone build.

Verification includes all 24 bridges in A4, Letter and A3 layouts, print/cancel lifecycle, public-only content, offline and standalone operation, mobile RTL, and rendered sample PDFs. Chrome is verified; other browser engines and physical printer drivers require local preview checks. See VERIFICATION.md for release results.
