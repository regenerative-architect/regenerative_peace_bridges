# Digital Peace Infrastructure — version 3.7.0

Open **Solution bridges** and choose **Digital Peace Infrastructure** in the category filter. The five independent pathways cover offline education hubs, community archives, local knowledge systems, mesh communication and resilience information portals. Each has prerequisites, pilot steps, risks, safeguards, a smaller first step and locally interpretable measures.

This family supports the Planetary Restoration Archive mission through community-owned learning and memory. It provides planning guidance; it does not install a learning server, archive storage system, mesh network or hosted portal. No automatic upload or external synchronization has been introduced.

## Use the existing learning cycle

1. Choose a bridge and open its planner. Seed its timeline and risks; budget equipment, power, facilitator time, maintenance and eventual replacement.
2. Keep separate evidence and community maturity reviews. All five begin at Conceptual / Proposed; institutional guidance does not demonstrate local outcomes.
3. Use Impact for applicable shared indicators such as participation, school attendance, service access and trust. The bridge-specific measures also cover recoverability, content freshness and network reliability. Record these in plan measures and contextual community observations; the fourteen structured impact indicators are unchanged.
4. Use Community knowledge to capture observations, lessons and unexpected outcomes. Fifteen new fictional prompts illustrate these pathways; prompts never become saved evidence automatically.
5. In **Evidence web**, inspect sources or use **System relationships** for proposed connections to education, governance, restoration and peace outcomes. State assumptions and limitations; no causal mapping is added automatically.
6. Use the solutions commons to request or offer knowledge, training, volunteers or equipment under the existing categories and sharing permissions.

## Community control and resilience

Agree access and reuse separately. Keep restricted knowledge and identifiable learner records outside shared plans. Preserve context, disagreement and knowledge-holder authority, including Indigenous data governance where relevant. Test backup restoration, fund maintenance and provide accessible non-digital alternatives. A mesh network does not inherently supply Internet access, encryption or emergency reliability. Offline information needs verification dates, expiry rules and a correction process.

## Compatibility and content maintenance

The additive category ID is digital. Bridge IDs are offline-education, community-archives, local-knowledge, mesh-communication and resilience-portals. Plan schema remains 3. Existing plans and bridge IDs remain valid; new bridges use existing lifecycle defaults and model validation. All five participate in cards, search, selectors, evidence links, backup/export and offline caching. New content uses existing reviewed content translation paths with English fallback.

Four official sources were opened and checked on 24 October 2026: Learning Equality’s About Kolibri, UNESCO’s documentary heritage overview, GIDA’s CARE Principles and Internet Society’s community network toolkit. They are guidance, not graded causal evidence. Existing accountability and listening guidance supports portal design. Each source keeps its own check date.
