Bridge Evolution System

Right now bridges are static.

I'd make them evolutionary.

Each bridge gains:

### Evidence maturity

- Conceptual
- Emerging
- Tested
- Replicated
- Context-specific Best Practice

### Community maturity

- Proposed
- Reviewed
- Adapted
- Implemented
- Locally Owned

Then users can track the lifecycle of a bridge.

Example:
```yaml
FMNR Bridge
Evidence: Tested
Community: Adapted
