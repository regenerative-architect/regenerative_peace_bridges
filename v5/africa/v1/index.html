Evidence into civilian recovery

Connect documented regenerative achievements to conflict-affected communities through context-sensitive, independently governed proposals. Prioritize children, safe water, food, education, health, land rights and community consent.

🔬 Implementation and research methods

Evidence grading and uncertainty

Grade A: independently replicated measured outcomes; B: credible evaluated implementation; C: case study or observational evidence; D: proposal or hypothesis. Do not present targets as achieved results. Record publication date, measurement period, geography, methodology and confounders.

Ecological transferability

Evaluate agroecological zone, rainfall, soil, native biodiversity, water budget, land tenure, grazing routes, invasive species risks, climate projections, labor and long-term maintenance. Prefer locally validated species and traditional knowledge.

Peace-positive evaluation

Use participatory conflict mapping, inclusive consent, stakeholder power analysis, dispute-resolution mechanisms and monitoring of unintended consequences. Consider distributional impacts across women, children, displaced residents, farmers and pastoralists.

Measurement and verification

Combine community-defined outcomes with remote sensing, field surveys, baseline comparisons and transparent uncertainty intervals. Track safe water functionality, dietary diversity, school attendance, vegetation survival, household income, complaints and conflict incidents without publishing identifying information.

Funding and procurement

Require beneficial-ownership due diligence, sanctions checks, independent procurement, safeguarding, audit trails and diversion controls. Do not trade humanitarian access for political endorsement or favorable publicity.

R&D opportunities

Research solar-powered decentralized water systems, farmer-managed natural regeneration, climate-security sensitivity tools, causal impact evaluation, satellite-based restoration verification, local-language knowledge transfer and conflict-sensitive cash/social protection. Treat emerging technologies as candidates for evaluation, not established solutions.

⚖️ Zero Harm & Anti-Coercion

Do not identify vulnerable individuals, publish precise locations of at-risk children or facilities, profile people for pressure, expose private information, or route support to armed actors. Humanitarian aid must be impartial, independent and never contingent on participation. Screen proposals for displacement, land dispossession, greenwashing, retaliation and resource conflict. Seek meaningful community consent and independent review.
